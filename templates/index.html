Understanding the condition

What is color blindness?

Color blindness, also called color vision deficiency, means the eyes see some colors differently or have trouble telling certain colors apart. It is usually inherited and present from birth. It is not a disease, it does not usually get worse, and most people with it see clearly in every other way.

About 1 in 12men are affected, according to commonly cited estimates
About 1 in 200women are affected, because the common types are passed through the X chromosome
Hundreds of millionsof people live with some form of it worldwide
Most common

Red and green deficiency

Includes protan types (reduced sensitivity to red light) and deutan types (reduced sensitivity to green light). Reds, greens, browns and oranges can look alike.

Rare

Blue and yellow deficiency

Called tritan types. Blues and greens, or yellows and pinks, can be confused. It affects men and women equally.

Very rare

Total color blindness

Called achromatopsia. The world is seen in shades of gray, and it is often joined by sensitivity to bright light.

Color blindness can also be acquired later in life through some eye diseases, aging, injuries or certain medicines. There is currently no cure for the inherited form, but tools and habits make daily life easier. An eye care professional can test for it, for example with the Ishihara plate test. This website is not a medical test.
Everyday life

How this tool can help

Color is used everywhere to carry information. When you cannot rely on seeing it, a quick spoken-style answer like "Olive Green, 70%" removes the guesswork.

Choosing clothes

Check whether a shirt and pants match, or find the pair of socks that really is navy and not black.

Food and shopping

Judge whether fruit is ripe, meat is cooked, or which package color is the one you wanted.

School and work

Read color coded charts, folders, wires, markers and labels with confidence.

Safety

Tell apart warning labels, indicator lights and colored cables before you touch or connect anything.

Hobbies and art

Pick the right paint, thread, beads or LEGO pieces by name instead of by guessing.

More than one color

Objects are rarely one color. The tool lists every main color on an object with its share, not just the biggest one.

Getting good results

How to use it

Start the camera

Press the button and allow camera access.

Fill the zone

Hold the object so it fills the green square. Use the slider to resize the zone.

Calibrate for accuracy

In the same light as your object, fill the zone with plain white paper and press Calibrate white.

Read the result

The main color is shown large, with every other color and its percentage, RGB and HEX code below it.

How it works

Built for CSC 126 Computer Vision with Python, OpenCV and Flask. The browser sends only the small sampling zone to the server, which analyzes it with classical OpenCV techniques.

Image operations

Cleaning the image

Blurring removes camera noise, and colors are converted from BGR to HSV and LAB so brightness and color can be judged separately.

Image segmentation

Isolating the object

Only the center zone is analyzed. A threshold mask removes glare, and morphology cleans the mask.

Analysis and transformation

Naming the colors

K-Means clustering groups similar pixels, close shades are merged, and each group is named from its hue, brightness and saturation.

Camera→Center zone→Blur→ HSV mask→LAB K-Means→Merge shades→Color names
